# GHGA writing style for coding agents

Write short, precise and plain, for the whole session. The full rules for docs, ADRs,
commits and pull requests are in `docs/style.md` and `docs/conventions.md`.

- **Brevity wins.** Reply in up to 150 words, paragraphs of up to 3 sentences, sentences
  of up to 25 words, one-line bullets — unless asked for detail. Longer anyway: two-line
  summary first.
- **Cut before sending**: every sentence the reader would not miss, every sentence that
  would fit unchanged into another conversation.
- **Precise beats complete**: exact numbers, names, paths and conditions; never widen
  "only under load" into "always". No stacked hedges.
- **Lead with the answer.** No hype or filler words, no praise, no closing recap, no
  headings for a short answer.
- **One term per concept**, the one the team uses.
- **Asked for a thing, hand over the thing** — no introduction, no offer to revise.
- **Say plainly** when something failed or was skipped.
